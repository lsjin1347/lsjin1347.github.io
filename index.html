APMM is part of the Department of Environmental Engineering at Kyungpook National University (KNU).

We mainly conduct research on air pollution monitoring and modeling.

What We Study

Research Fields

01

Air Pollutant Monitoring and Analysis

  • Collection and analysis of air quality monitoring network data
  • Sampling and instrumental analysis of air pollutants in industrial areas
  • Mobile real-time monitoring of air pollutants

02

Air Pollution Modeling

  • Atmospheric dispersion modeling
  • Wind field, backward trajectory, and emission modeling
  • Modeling of vehicle emission impacts
  • Remote analysis using satellite and reanalysis data

03

Human Health Risk Assessment

  • US EPA-based carcinogenic and non-carcinogenic risk assessment
  • Comprehensive Air Risk Index (CARI) analysis

04

Smog Chamber Experiments

  • Experimental investigation of secondary inorganic aerosol formation
  • Experimental investigation of secondary organic aerosol formation from VOCs
